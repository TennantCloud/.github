## Hey, we're Tennant

We're building an opinionated cloud for all your compute and storage, optimized around how you and your agents work. Install apps and databases, share work across agents, and pay only for the compute you use.

### What we're building

- **Sandboxes** — Firecracker-powered micro VMs that boot fast and die clean. Each one gets its own filesystem, network, and resource limits.
- **Templates & Snapshots** — Define your environment once, reuse it everywhere. Pause a running sandbox and resume it later exactly where you left off.
- **SDK & CLI** — Programmatic control over your environments. Create, deploy, execute, and tear down from code or the terminal.
- **GitHub Integration** — Connect repos, trigger builds, and manage deployments from your existing workflow.
